Inferred · the pink group on the wheel

Options that only exist because it read the file

A format list says what a JPG can become. It cannot say that this JPG is a photographed form, a table, or a scan of a letter. ShapeShifter looks inside, finds out, and offers what makes sense.

New in 2.6 · the grey group on the wheel

Properties: see and change what is hidden inside a file

Every photo carries its camera, date and often its GPS position. Every document carries an author. PROPERTIES shows all of it in one window, lets you change or remove any of it, and leaves the picture, the text and the sound alone.

Safe by default

It refuses what could hurt

Programs and scripts, password-protected files, archives that try to write outside their folder, zip bombs, files that claim to be one thing and are another. Private keys can only be re-encoded, never turned into documents. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The fine print

Honest limits

  • Old .doc, .xls and .ppt files give their text, tables and values, not formatting or pictures. LibreOffice does them better when installed.
  • Slides are redrawn from their shapes, so fonts, effects and animations are simplified. Camera RAW uses the camera's embedded full-size preview, not developed sensor data.
  • Not covered: Parquet, Arrow, Avro, HDF5, DJVU, DWG, WOFF2, Apple Pages, Numbers and Keynote, Access, MIDI, Flash.
  • The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn: choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Figures on this page come from the conversion graph of ShapeShifter 2.6.0 on a machine with ffmpeg and pandoc installed; LibreOffice and 7-Zip were not, so a few more routes appear when they are.
