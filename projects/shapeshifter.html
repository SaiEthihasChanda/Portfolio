What it does

[ FEATURES ]
01

The wheel

Right-click › Convert to… or press Ctrl+Alt+C. A wheel of everything the file can become appears under your mouse. Past 12 options, they spread over rotating rings.

02

In place

holiday.jpg becomes holiday.png in the same folder, and the original goes to the Recycle Bin.

03

Knows what a file really is

It reads byte signatures, containers, text and ffmpeg's media probe, so renamed or extension-less files still work.

04

A graph of conversions

Every file type is a node and every step an edge, so multi-step routes like DOCX → PDF → PNG are found automatically.

05

Learns reverse conversions

Convert A → B once and it remembers that B → A is wanted, with a warning badge when the route loses something.

06

Documents keep their pictures

PDF and Word output keeps images, links, tables, lists and non-Latin text. Nothing is silently dropped.
