What it does

[ FEATURES ]
01

Activity

Add income and expenses with an amount and a trackable. See the day's total income, expenses and net flow at a glance.

02

Trackables

Create recurring monthly or yearly items and link each to a bank account or card.

03

Tracker

Mark recurring items done for the month. Ticking one adds the activity for you, with a progress percentage.

04

Analytics

Filter by time range, account or trackable, then read income, expenses, net flow, the expense/income ratio and category breakdowns.

05

Smart about card bills

Credit-card bills are treated as self-transfers, so they never inflate your totals.

06

Private by design

Each user signs in and only ever sees their own data, synced through Firebase.
